# RenoHab — Product context

## Register
brand

## Product purpose
renohab.fr is the marketing and content site of RenoHab, an approved *Accompagnateur Rénov'* (MAR). It explains French energy-renovation rules and subsidies (MaPrimeRénov', CEE, éco-PTZ, TVA 5,5 %, DPE) and converts visitors into leads: the audit/accompaniment form, the Kelvin aid simulator, and heat-pump quote requests (/pompe-a-chaleur). A daily SEO article feeds /blog.

## Target users
French homeowners (and landlords) facing a renovation decision: often 40–70, not technical, worried about cost, scams and paperwork. They arrive from Google on a specific question (an aid, a DPE deadline) and need to trust the source before leaving their contact details.

## Brand personality
Expert and reassuring. Precise, sourced, up to date (dates and decree numbers matter), closer to a trustworthy public-service guide than to a flashy startup, yet warmer and more human. Clarity and confidence over wow-effect.

## Anti-references
- Lead-gen "arnaque" sites: countdown banners, "100 % gratuit !", stock-photo overload, aggressive pop-ups.
- Generic SaaS landing pages: hero metrics, identical icon-card grids, gradient text.
- Title Case Headings (un-French) and emoji used as UI.

## Strategic principles
1. Trust before conversion: show credentials (MAR agréé, artisans RGE, sources dated) near every call to action.
2. Content is the product: regulatory text stays accurate; design makes it scannable, never hides it.
3. One clear next step per section (simulate, request an audit, PAC quote).
4. Fast on mobile: most traffic is mobile search; nothing heavy loads before it is needed.
5. SEO-safe: every route is pre-rendered by react-snap; markup must hydrate identically.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Older audience: body text ≥ 16px, strong contrast, large tap targets, honour prefers-reduced-motion.
